# Final live QA

These are the essential manual RuneLite smoke checks. Synthetic coverage can
exercise unavailable account types, but it does not replace one real-client
login before submission.

- [ ] Enable Compass before a fresh login. Confirm the sidebar appears without
  a serious freeze and detects the character, membership, and account mode.
- [ ] Inspect one ordinary skilling DO NEXT. METHOD, BRING, WHERE, DO, PROGRESS,
  and WHY must be nonblank, coherent, and immediately executable.
- [ ] Inspect one distant multi-stage skill target. Confirm only the legal
  current stage is shown, then gain its transition level and confirm the plan
  advances while retaining the original goal.
- [ ] On any available Main/Iron/UIM accounts, confirm Main may buy a verified
  shortfall, Iron does not receive GE instructions, and UIM does not receive a
  conventional-bank loop. Automated scenarios cover account types unavailable
  to the maintainer.
- [ ] Open Details and Method Guidance together and separately. Confirm their
  method, supplies, location, action, progress, and risk text agree with the
  sidebar and do not clip at Standard, Large, or Extra-large text.
- [ ] Use Later, Not Today, and Dislike. Each should rotate immediately without
  looping back; reset learned feedback once and confirm recommendations recover.
- [ ] Gain XP and inspect Progress: session/skill XP, active time, rate/ETA after
  stabilization, chart order, milestones, and NOW/NEXT/TARGET should update once.
- [ ] Log out and switch character. No prior character, inventory assumption,
  progress, feedback, or overlay text may appear on the new account.
- [ ] Disable and re-enable the plugin, reopen the sidebar, and toggle overlays.
  Confirm one navigation button, one instance of each overlay, and no exception.
- [ ] Leave Compass running through ordinary inventory, bank, quest, Slayer,
  varbit, and POH events. Confirm there is no repeated rerank loop or client lag.
