# Human RuneLite gameplay checklist

These checks require a person playing through RuneLite. They are not marked
passed by repository automation.

- Login and confirm initial DO NEXT is nonblank; logout/login and world-hop.
- Open and close the bank; change inventory and equipment; verify prompt refresh without stale UI.
- Verify carried Rune pouch contents, spellbook, and prayer evidence remain conservative when unobserved.
- Complete a quest prerequisite and quest; test the Quest Helper handoff.
- Receive, change, and complete a Slayer assignment.
- Exercise a ready recurring activity and confirm it competes without repeating excessively.
- Use Later, Not Today, Dislike, and Do This; confirm immediate rotation and persistence.
- Change strategy, session intent, goal, and training preference.
- Exercise specific PvM PREPARE and a supported VERIFIED READY case.
- Exercise gear, diary, transport, clue, and STASH preparation goals.
- On applicable accounts, observe GIM Group Storage and UIM retrieval-only storage.
- Switch accounts and confirm goals, feedback, observations, and preferences do not leak.
- If practical, rename an RSN while retaining the same account hash and confirm identity continuity.
- Stress sidebar wrapping, Details, Method Guidance, UI scaling, event bursts, and performance.
- Throughout the run, confirm DO NEXT never becomes blank.
