# Moons of Peril — Grind Ready review

Reviewed 2026-11-13 for GitHub issue #10.

- `Perilous Moons`, OSRS Wiki revision `15399389`: mandatory levels are 48 Slayer, 20 Hunter, 20 Fishing, 20 Runecraft, and 10 Construction; `Twilight's Promise` is the direct quest prerequisite. The quest unlocks the Moons encounter. Recommended combat levels are guidance, not access requirements. Repeatable dungeon supplies scale with account skills: the reviewed preparation floor is 60 Cooking, 55 Herblore, and either 50 Hunter or 60 Fishing.
- `Moons of Peril/Strategies`, OSRS Wiki revision `15399414`: all three bosses use melee but favour different attack styles (stab, crush, and slash). Neypotzli provides a self-contained food and moonlight-potion preparation loop. Defensive equipment and a sustainable multi-style setup affect repeat farming readiness.
- `Money making guide/Moons of Peril`, OSRS Wiki revision `15399704`: repeat farming recommends 70+ Attack, Strength, and Defence plus strong style-appropriate weapons and high-defence armour. Compass uses the three combat levels as a practical repeat-readiness floor, not a quest or encounter access requirement. Exact gear suitability remains a separate observed-evidence check.

Runtime policy: completion of `Perilous Moons` proves access only. Compass requires separately observed preparation evidence before it may recommend a repeatable run, and it does not infer player execution skill or promise successful kills. Main, Iron, Group Iron, and UIM accounts use the same self-contained dungeon guidance without assumed GE, Group Storage, or bank access; Hardcore variants are suppressed, and unknown account modes fail closed at the final irreversible-work safety gate.
